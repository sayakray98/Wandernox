🌏 Singapore Delight – 5 Days Itinerary

Embark on a captivating journey through Singapore — from the legendary Merlion Park and Marina Bay to Sentosa Island, Universal Studios, and the breathtaking Gardens by the Bay.

Inclusions
  • 4 Nights' accommodation at Aqueen Hotel Lavender or similar
  • Meals on Breakfast basis at the Hotel
  • Return Airport Transfers
  • All tours and transfers on SIC basis
  • Cable Car 1-Way, Wings of Time 2nd Show
  • Luge and Sky Rides – 2 Rounds in Sentosa
  • Garden by the Bay and Marina Bay Sky Pass
Day-Wise Itinerary
1🗓️Arrival in Singapore – Welcome to the Lion City
Arrive at the award-winning Changi International Airport. Our local representative will greet you and assist with your transfer to the hotel. The rest of the day is free to unwind — stroll down Orchard Road, visit Bugis Street Market, or enjoy local delicacies at a hawker centre.
2🏙️Iconic Singapore City Tour & Sentosa Island Escapade
After breakfast, enjoy a panoramic half-day city tour visiting Merlion Park, Orchard Road, and the culturally rich lanes of Chinatown. In the afternoon, head to Sentosa Island for a scenic one-way cable car ride, followed by Madame Tussauds Singapore (4-in-1 experience: wax museum, Images of Singapore LIVE, Spirit of Singapore boat ride, Marvel 4D). As the sun sets, experience the enchanting Wings of Time night show.
3🎢Full-Day Thrill at Universal Studios Singapore
After breakfast, enjoy a full day at Universal Studios Singapore — Southeast Asia's first Hollywood-themed theme park. Explore 7 themed zones: Hollywood Boulevard, New York, Sci-Fi City, Ancient Egypt, The Lost World, Far Far Away, and Madagascar. Transformers 3D, Revenge of the Mummy, Shrek 4D, and much more await!
4🌸Gardens by the Bay & Marina Bay Sands SkyPark
Morning at the world-renowned Gardens by the Bay — explore the Flower Dome (world's largest glass greenhouse) and the misty Cloud Forest with its 35-meter indoor waterfall. In the evening, head to the Marina Bay Sands SkyPark on the 57th floor for breathtaking 360-degree panoramic views of Singapore's skyline.
5✈️Farewell Singapore – Departure with Beautiful Memories
Enjoy a relaxed breakfast at the hotel. After checking out, our team will assist with a comfortable transfer to Changi International Airport. Bid farewell to the Lion City with a heart full of memories!

🌏 Singapore & Malaysia Discovery – 5 Days, 4 Nights

Uncover the charm of Southeast Asia — Singapore's modern wonders and the cultural treasures of Kuala Lumpur and Genting Highlands, all in one seamless journey.

Inclusions
  • 2 Nights' accommodation in Singapore at Hotel Fortuna or similar
  • 2 Nights' accommodation in Kuala Lumpur at Ibis Styles Fraser Business or similar
  • Meals on Breakfast basis at the Hotel
  • Return Airport Transfers & All Transfers on SIC basis
  • Night Safari Singapore
  • Sentosa Island – Cable Car, Madame Tussauds, Wings of Time
  • KL City Tour, Petronas, Genting Highlands & Batu Caves
Day-Wise Itinerary
1🛬Arrive Singapore – Evening Night Safari
Arrive at Changi Airport, check-in at hotel. In the evening, experience the world's first nocturnal zoo — Singapore Night Safari. Guided tram through 7 geographic zones, spot tigers, leopards, elephants and more under the moonlight.
2🏙️Singapore City Highlights & Sentosa Island Escape
Half-day city tour: Merlion Park, Orchard Road, Chinatown. Then Sentosa Island — cable car ride, Madame Tussauds 4-in-1, and the breathtaking Wings of Time show.
3🏙️Transfer to Kuala Lumpur – Explore Malaysia's Capital
After breakfast, transfer to KL. Visit Petronas Twin Towers, King's Palace, National Monument, National Mosque, Jamek Mosque, Merdeka Square, and National Museum.
4🏞️Genting Highlands – Batu Caves & Mountain Fun
Full-day to Genting Highlands — stop at sacred Batu Caves and the golden Lord Murugan statue, then ride the Genting Skyway Cable Car to the summit. Explore Skytropolis Indoor Theme Park, cool-climate gardens and views.
5✈️Farewell Kuala Lumpur – Departure with Beautiful Memories
Relaxed final breakfast, check-out, comfortable transfer to Kuala Lumpur International Airport (KLIA). Safe travels!

🌟 Top 15 Must-Do Activities in Singapore

Your ultimate guide to unforgettable experiences in the Lion City — from futuristic gardens to thrilling theme parks.

1
Gardens by the Bay

Supertree Grove, Cloud Forest, and the Flower Dome — where nature meets innovation.

2
Universal Studios Singapore

Southeast Asia's first Hollywood-themed park — 7 zones, epic rides, live shows.

3
Singapore Zoo

Open-concept rainforest zoo with 2,800+ animals. Don't miss Jungle Breakfast!

4
Marina Bay Sands

SkyPark 360° views, luxury shopping, world-class dining, and the Spectra light show.

5
Sentosa Island

S.E.A. Aquarium, Adventure Cove Waterpark, beaches, and Wings of Time show.

6
Singapore Flyer

165-meter observation wheel with panoramic city views — perfect at sunset.

7
Night Safari

World's first nocturnal wildlife park — tram ride, walking trails, and animal encounters.

8
Jewel Changi Airport

World's tallest indoor waterfall (40m), lush forest valley, shopping, and Canopy Park.

9
Merlion Park

Singapore's most iconic landmark — perfect photos, waterfront views, and city energy.

10
Chinatown

Buddha Tooth Relic Temple, Maxwell Food Centre, and vibrant street markets.

11
Little India

Vibrant colors, spice markets, Sri Veeramakaliamman Temple, and authentic cuisine.

12
Clarke Quay

Riverside dining, river cruises, live music, and Singapore's most vibrant nightlife.

13
Singapore Botanic Gardens

UNESCO Heritage site with the National Orchid Garden and tranquil Swan Lake.

14
National Museum

Singapore's oldest museum — immersive galleries, multimedia exhibits, and stunning architecture.

15
S.E.A. Aquarium

100,000+ marine animals — the Open Ocean Habitat, Shark Seas, and dazzling coral reefs.
