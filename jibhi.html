🏔️ Discover the Magic of Jibhi

Nestled in the Tirthan Valley of Himachal Pradesh, Jibhi is a paradise for those seeking tranquility amid breathtaking Himalayan landscapes. Ancient temples, lush deodar forests, crystal-clear rivers, and starlit skies await you.

This Wandernox exclusive package is designed to immerse you in the raw beauty of the mountains — with handpicked homestays, curated treks, and unforgettable sunsets over the Great Himalayan National Park.

🏕️ Jibhi Escape Package

Day-Wise Itinerary
1Delhi to Jibhi – Overnight Volvo Journey

Board the comfortable Volvo bus from Delhi ISBT in the evening. Settle in for an overnight journey through the Punjab plains and into the foothills of Himachal Pradesh. Wake up to misty mountain views!

2Arrive Jibhi – Waterfall Trek & Bonfire

Arrive at Aut and transfer to Jibhi. Check into your cozy mountain homestay. After lunch, embark on a guided trek to the stunning Jibhi Waterfall. Evening bonfire with snacks and music under the stars!

3Jalori Pass – Serolsar Lake & Ancient Temple

Full-day excursion to Jalori Pass (10,800 ft) — trek to the mystical Serolsar Lake surrounded by thick forests and ancient legends. Visit the Raghupur Fort ruins with panoramic valley views. Return for dinner and storytelling.

4Departure – Farewell to the Mountains

Early morning sunrise viewing, relaxed breakfast, check-out and transfer back to Aut for your return Volvo to Delhi. Arrive Delhi by late evening with a heart full of mountain memories!

Note: Itinerary may vary based on weather and road conditions. Wandernox reserves the right to modify the itinerary for safety reasons.
