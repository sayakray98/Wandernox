FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about our travel services, booking process, and policies.

Booking a tour with Wandernox is easy! Simply browse our available tours on the Tours page, select your preferred package, and click "Book Now." You'll be guided through a seamless online booking process where you can choose your dates, number of travelers, and any add-ons. Once your booking is confirmed, you'll receive a detailed itinerary and all essential travel information via email within 24 hours.

We offer a flexible cancellation policy to give you peace of mind. Cancellations made up to 30 days before the departure date are eligible for a full refund. Cancellations within 15–30 days receive a 75% refund, and those within 7–14 days receive a 50% refund. For cancellations made less than 7 days before departure, a 25% refund applies. We understand that plans can change, and we always try to work with our travelers to find the best solution.

Yes, we offer comprehensive travel insurance through our trusted insurance partners. Our plans cover trip cancellations, medical emergencies, lost luggage, flight delays, and more. We highly recommend adding travel insurance to your booking for complete peace of mind. Insurance can be added during the booking process or up to 14 days before your departure date. Our team can also help you choose the right coverage based on your destination and travel needs.

Absolutely! Every trip can be tailored to your preferences. Whether you want to extend your stay at a particular destination, swap activities, upgrade accommodations, or add private excursions — our travel experts will work closely with you to craft the perfect itinerary. Simply select the "Custom Trip" option in our contact form or reach out to our team directly. We specialize in creating bespoke travel experiences that match your unique style and interests.

We accept a wide range of payment methods for your convenience, including Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and PayPal. We also support bank transfers for larger bookings. All transactions are processed through secure, encrypted payment gateways to ensure your financial information remains protected. For group bookings or premium packages, we also offer flexible installment plans — just ask our team for more details.
